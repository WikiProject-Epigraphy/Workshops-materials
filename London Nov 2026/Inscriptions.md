
# List of inscriptions for hands-on

## RIB

* [RIB 154](https://romaninscriptionsofbritain.org/inscriptions/154): Curse upon thief of ‘Vilbia’
* [RIB 221](https://romaninscriptionsofbritain.org/inscriptions/221): Curse upon Tacita

## Inscriptions of Aphrodisias
* [IAph12.318](https://insaph.kcl.ac.uk/iaph2007/iAph120318.html): Building inscription: restoration of building by ?Eusebes
* [12.602](https://insaph.kcl.ac.uk/iaph2007/iAph120602.html): Funerary honours for Geis and Heraios

## Mappola
* [MPL00040](https://db.mappola.eu/r/Inscriptions/show/40): Fragmentary verse inscription from Phoebiana
* [MPL00146](https://db.mappola.eu/r/Inscriptions/show/146):Stele for Veiania Hospita and L. Fabricius Clemens

* [EDR016040]([EDR015860](https://edr.lad-sapienza.it/edr_programmi/res_complex_comune.php?do=book&id_nr=EDR016040&fo_antik=&fo_modern=&Bibliografia%5B%5D=&Testo=&boolTesto=AND&Testo2=&bool=AND&ordinamento=id_nr&javasi=javascriptsi&se_foto=tutte&lang=it)): Funerary Inscription of a magister vici

* [IGBulg_1955](https://telamon.uni-sofia.bg/en/epi/view_ins/IGBulg_1955): Funerary inscription for Dilles
* [IGBulg_1421](https://telamon.uni-sofia.bg/en/epi/view_ins/IGBulg_1421): Dedication by Hadrianius Demetrianus and Hadrianius Asclepiades
